How to Make Lumpia

Master the art of crafting the perfect crispy, golden-brown Filipino spring rolls right at home.

Ingredients

  • Lumpia wrappers
  • 3 cups cooking oil (for frying)
  • Carrots (finely chopped)
  • Onions (minced)
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Extra cooking oil (for sautéing)

The procedure in cooking lumpia is simple.
There are 3 major steps:

  1. Mix the filling ingredients

    Combine all your filling ingredients thoroughly in a bowl. Mix them altogether until the profile is well blended and uniform.

  2. Wrap it flawlessly

    The next step is to wrap the lumpia mixture tightly. You can use common egg roll wrappers, or choose traditional Filipino lumpia wrappers for an authentic, extra-shatteringly crispy crunch.

  3. Fry until perfectly crispy

    The final stage is deep frying to golden perfection. Deep frying provides an even, beautiful crunch across the entire roll.

💡

The Secret to Frying Perfect Lumpia

Deep frying lumpia has a clever trick to ensure the inside filling cooks completely through before the outer wrapper burns: Temperature control.

Never drop lumpia into smoking hot oil! This burns the shell while leaving the meat raw. Start frying right when your oil begins warming up (around 250°F). Let it cook slowly; the wrapper will brown gradually. When the lumpia floats to the top, it is cooked perfectly!

Dipping Options: Pair your masterpiece with sweet and sour sauce, spicy spiced vinegar, or classic banana ketchup!

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. 01
    Start by sautéing garlic and onion until fragrant — this builds the savory flavor base.
  2. 02
    Add ground meat to your pan and cook thoroughly until it is beautifully browned.
  3. 03
    Mix in the carrots, soy sauce, salt, and pepper. Stir well so everything balances out.
  4. 04
    Remove the filling mix from heat and let it cool down fully — this prevents your wrappers from tearing!
  5. 05
    Place a spoonful of filling onto a wrapper and roll it tightly, tucking the edges neatly like a little gift.
  6. 06
    Pour your 3 cups of frying oil into a deep pan and set to moderate heat.
  7. 07
    Gently slide them in and fry your lumpia slowly until they turn an even, golden-brown color.
  8. 08
    Serve hot out of the pan alongside your dipping sauce of choice for maximum crispy happiness!
