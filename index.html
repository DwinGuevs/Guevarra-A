ABOUT me

HI, I'M ALDWIN

I'm Aldwin Raven L. Guevarra, a BSIT student specializing in Mobile & Web Applications, and a passionate photographer and videographer. With a strong foundation in IT and a creative eye for visual storytelling, I blend technical expertise with artistic vision to capture moments that matter.

I hold a BS in Information Technology (Mobile & Web Applications) from National University Clark, along with an Aircraft Maintenance Technology diploma and TVL-ICT training. My certifications include CompTIA ITF, Microsoft Office Specialist 365 Apps, and IT Specialist (Databases), (HTML and CSS).

Beyond my technical work, I'm also passionate about photography and videography — accepting freelance projects and covering events with professional photo and video coverage. Whether it's data-driven solutions or creative storytelling, I bring precision, adaptability, and artistry to every project.
